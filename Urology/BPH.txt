BPH most commonly arises from which zone of the prostate?
A) Transitional zone
B) Peripheral zone
C) Central zone
D) Anterior fibromuscular stroma
E) Capsular zone
Correct Answer: A) Transitional zone
Explanation: BPH develops in the transitional zone, whereas prostate cancer arises predominantly (70–80%) from the peripheral zone.

In uncomplicated bladder outflow obstruction due to BPH, which finding is typical?
A) Rapid symptomatic improvement with finasteride
B) Symptom relief with alpha-1 adrenoceptor agonists
C) Reduced voiding pressure
D) Voiding flow rate less than 15 mL/sec
E) Prostatectomy as first-line treatment
Correct Answer: D) Voiding flow rate less than 15 mL/sec
Explanation: A maximum flow rate below 15 mL/sec (with <10 mL/sec strongly suggestive) is typical of obstruction. Finasteride takes 6+ months to work, and alpha-blockers (not agonists) relieve symptoms.
